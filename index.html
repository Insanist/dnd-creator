D&D 5e (2024) Character Creator

Levels 1-5 · Player's Handbook species, backgrounds, classes and subclasses.

Step 1: The basics

Start with who your character is. Species gives traits like darkvision, Background gives two skills and a starting feat, Class is your main job in the party. Not sure? Press the sparkle button to fill in a good beginner build for your class.

Sets ability scores, subclass (level 3+), skills, special choices and spells to beginner-friendly picks. You can change anything afterwards.

Have a character code from a friend (or an earlier save)? Load it here

Step 2: Ability scores

Six numbers describe your strengths. 10 is average, and every 2 points above that adds +1 to your rolls. Put your biggest number in your class's main stat. "Use recommended" does this for you; you can still tweak it.

Step 3: Skills

Skills are what you are trained in outside combat: sneaking, persuading, spotting danger, and so on. Pick the number your class allows; your background already gave you two.

Step 4: Special choices

Some classes, species and feats have extra choices that shape how your character plays. The defaults are fine to start with; read the short description under each option.

Step 5: Starting gear

Pick a ready-made kit of armor and weapons for your class. Your armor class and attack bonuses are worked out for you.

Your AC and attack bonuses are worked out from the kit for you.

Step 6: Spells

Spells are your magic. Cantrips can be cast all day for free; leveled spells use up spell slots. Starred spells are good first picks for your class.

Step 7: Notes, gear, story

Optional: extra gear, house-rule notes and your character's story. Everything here is added to the PDF.

Step 8: Review and download

Check the list below, fix anything flagged, then download your sheet. You can come back and change things any time.
